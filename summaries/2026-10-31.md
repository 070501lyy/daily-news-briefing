**Binance 遭史上最大加密黑客攻击，损失 63 亿美元 / Binance hit by largest crypto heist ever, $6.3 billion stolen**

全球最大加密货币交易所 Binance 遭黑客攻击，约 63 亿美元资产被转移，成为有记录以来规模最大的加密货币盗窃案。两家区块链分析公司 TRM Labs 和 Chainalysis 均以"高置信度"指认朝鲜黑客组织 Lazarus Group（又称 TraderTraitor）涉案。Binance 表示已冻结约一半被盗资产，并承诺用公司资金覆盖全部损失。攻击据称始于一名工程师被入侵的笔记本电脑，黑客通过控制提现授权系统在几分钟内转走数十亿美元。比特币价格下跌约 5% 至 76,800 美元。

Binance, the world's largest crypto exchange, was hit by what appears to be the largest cryptocurrency heist on record, with roughly $6.3 billion in assets moved. Two blockchain analytics firms, TRM Labs and Chainalysis, identified North Korea's Lazarus Group (also known as TraderTraitor) as the culprit with "high confidence." Binance says it has frozen about half the stolen funds and pledged to cover all losses with company money. The attack is believed to have started with a compromised engineer's laptop, with hackers gaining control of a withdrawal authorization system to move billions in minutes. Bitcoin fell about 5% to $76,800.

**Nvidia 考虑为 Nscale 的西弗吉尼亚 AI 数据中心提供债务担保 / Nvidia weighs co-signing debt for Nscale's West Virginia AI data center**

Nvidia 正洽谈为 AI 云服务商 Nscale 在西弗吉尼亚州的数据中心提供数十亿美元债务担保，该数据中心已与 Anthropic 签下 446 亿美元的首栋建筑合同。这种"剩余价值担保"安排意味着若 Nscale 违约，Nvidia 将临时接管设施并寻找买家。此举凸显 AI 行业为维持大规模建设计划正承受越来越大的融资压力，Nscale 计划以高达 350 亿美元估值上市。

Nvidia is in talks to guarantee billions in debt financing for AI cloud provider Nscale's planned West Virginia data center, which has signed a $44.6 billion contract with Anthropic for its first building alone. The "residual value guarantee" arrangement would see Nvidia temporarily take over the facility and find a buyer if Nscale defaults. The move underscores the mounting financing pressure on the AI industry to keep its buildout plans afloat, as Nscale seeks to go public at a valuation of up to $35 billion.

Anthropic officially filed its S-1, set to be the largest IPO in history and the first major US public company focused solely on AI software. Its valuation jumped from $183 billion just over a year ago to $965 billion in its latest round, with some expecting close to $2 trillion once it starts trading.
