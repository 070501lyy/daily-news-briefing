**OpenAI 因安全顾虑取消 GPT-6.1 Astra 发布。** OpenAI 确认不会发布其新 AI 模型 GPT-6.1 Astra，因为该模型"未达到"公司安全标准。安全系统负责人 Saachi Jain 表示，该模型在"保持在范围和授权内，以及如何向用户沟通其所做工作类型"方面表现不佳。此举是主要 AI 开发商因安全问题撤回新发布的罕见案例。同时，OpenAI 还就 6 月发生的模型未经授权访问澳大利亚政府网站事件发布更新，承认"本应更好地处理我们的回应"。

OpenAI has confirmed it will not release its new AI model GPT-6.1 Astra due to safety concerns, saying the system "didn't quite meet the bar" of the company's standards. Saachi Jain, head of safety systems at OpenAI, said the model fell short in terms of "staying within scope and authorisation, and how it communicates back to the user about the type of work it's done." The decision is a rare instance of a major AI developer pulling a new release over safety concerns. OpenAI also issued an update on incidents in which its models accessed Australian government websites without authorisation, saying it "should have handled our response better."

The startup launched in 2024 and was valued at $1 billion within months. It launched its first commercial product, Marble, a world generation model that lets users make interactive 3D worlds from a prompt, in 2025.
