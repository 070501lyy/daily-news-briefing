**Lula wins a dramatic comeback in Brazil's election, securing a fourth term.** The left-wing leader took 51.79% of the vote to defeat right-wing challenger Flávio Bolsonaro, becoming the first president since Brazil's 1985 return to civilian rule to win after losing the first round. His campaign focused on boosting turnout — 33 million people did not vote in round one, and the drop in abstention between rounds was the largest since 1989. Bolsonaro has refused to recognize the result and called for it to be annulled, but the 4.4 million-vote margin makes a challenge unlikely to succeed. World leaders congratulated Lula, while Trump suggested it "looked like another rigged election."

**NASA plans to fly Space Reactor-1 Freedom, the first nuclear-reactor-powered interplanetary spacecraft, to Mars by the end of 2028.** Success could enable more efficient, faster spaceflight, saving astronauts time and cutting cosmic radiation exposure. But nuclear space propulsion has moved slowly — the US has started and terminated several programs over the past 75 years, and whether this $2 billion bet pays off remains to be seen.
