## 今日要点 / Today's Highlights

**COP31气候大会无果而终，成为联合国史上最长气候谈判。** 在土耳其安塔利亚举行的COP31上，各国因化石燃料表述和资金承诺问题陷入严重分歧，最终未能达成核心政治协议。欧盟、太平洋岛国等因最终草案只字未提"化石燃料"而拒绝签署，贫困国家则对富裕国家缺乏明确资金承诺表示不满。上一次COP主要协议如此破裂还要追溯到2009年哥本哈根。联合国秘书长古特雷斯称"深感失望"，直指"强大的化石燃料利益集团"照搬烟草业套路。

The COP31 climate summit in Antalya, Turkey, ended without a major deal, becoming the longest UN climate conference in history. Deep divisions over fossil fuel language and financial commitments stalled the core political agreement. The EU, Pacific island nations and others rejected the final draft because it failed to mention fossil fuels at all, while poorer countries objected to the absence of clear financial commitments from rich nations. The last time a COP's main agreement collapsed like this was Copenhagen in 2009. UN Secretary General António Guterres said he was "deeply disappointed," taking aim at "powerful fossil fuel interests" he said were taking "a page from Big Tobacco's playbook."

**英国NHS启动全球最大规模AI乳腺癌筛查试验。** EDITH试验将邀请近70万名50至71岁女性参与，用AI替代两名人类专家中的一位来审阅乳腺X光片（mammogram）。该试验耗资1100万英镑，由华威大学、剑桥大学等牵头，预计持续约三年，有四家AI公司参与。研究者强调需确保AI不会导致不必要的过度诊断。

The UK's NHS has launched the world's largest trial of AI in breast cancer screening. The EDITH trial will invite nearly 700,000 women aged 50 to 71, using AI to replace one of the two human experts reviewing mammograms. The £11 million trial is led by the University of Warwick, University of Cambridge and others, expected to last about three years, with four AI companies involved. Researchers stressed the need to ensure AI doesn't cause unnecessary overdiagnosis.

**Anthropic完成史上最大IPO，估值约1.5万亿美元。** 据TechCrunch报道，Anthropic于周三通过IPO募资813亿美元，成为有史以来最大规模IPO，完全稀释估值约1.5万亿美元。早期投资者Notable Capital、新加坡GIC和Baillie Gifford均获得巨额账面回报，其中Notable约获15倍回报。GIC在三轮融资中累计投资超45亿美元，持股约占IPO后公司1%。

Anthropic raised $81.3 billion in the largest IPO in history on Wednesday, reaching a fully diluted valuation of about $1.5 trillion, according to TechCrunch. Early backers including Notable Capital, Singapore's GIC and Baillie Gifford are sitting on massive paper windfalls—Notable saw roughly a 15x return. GIC invested more than $4.5 billion across three rounds, amassing a stake equal to about 1% of the company after its IPO.

**Anthropic员工称用Claude证明黎曼猜想，数学家呼吁谨慎验证。** Anthropic数学家Levent Alpöge称花约六周用尚未公开的Claude版本构建了黎曼猜想的详细证明，目前公布的是以Lean语言撰写的证明，估计人类可读版本将超25万页。知名数学家陶哲轩表示验证仍需时间。此事发生在Anthropic上市次日，时机引发争议，亦有数学学者批评AI公司大肆宣扬重大解法"对数学科学有害"。

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge claims he used an unreleased version of Claude over about six weeks to build a detailed proof of the Riemann hypothesis, publishing it in the Lean proof language—estimated at over 250,000 human-readable pages. Star mathematician Terence Tao said verification will take time. Coming the day after Anthropic's IPO, the timing drew scrutiny, and some academics warned that AI firms trumpeting major solutions is "harmful to mathematical science."

**研究揭示AI供应链大规模泄露用户敏感数据。** Ars Technica报道，研究人员在经由AI服务网络发送的请求中植入200万个数字"绊线"，发现整个生态系统如筛子般泄露高度敏感数据。许多AI应用使用OpenRouter、Vercel等"AI路由器"将prompt转发给推理引擎。后续更新显示，OpenRouter已通知部分客户其prompt可能被读取，涉及发往中国模型提供商MiniMax的请求，并已移除相关端点的"零数据保留"标签。

Researchers planted 2 million digital tripwires in requests sent through a sprawling network of AI services and found an ecosystem leaking highly sensitive data "like a sieve," Ars Technica reported. Many AI apps use "AI routers" like OpenRouter and Vercel to route prompts to inference engines. In an update, OpenRouter notified some customers their prompts may have been read, involving requests to China-based model provider MiniMax, and removed a "Zero Data Retention" label from affected endpoints.
