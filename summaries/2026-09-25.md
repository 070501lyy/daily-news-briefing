**NHS launches rapid brain tumour genomic test, cutting diagnosis from weeks to two hours**。Using Oxford Nanopore sequencing, the test classifies tumours while surgery is still underway. It already identified a grade 4 glioblastoma in a 55-year-old patient mid-operation. NHS England will pilot at five specialist centres in Nottingham, Birmingham, London, and Newcastle.

**Lightspeed targets $250M for new India fund focused entirely on early-stage AI**。This is the first time Lightspeed's India funds are on the same fundraising cycle as its global funds. The new vehicle is half the size of its $500M predecessor but already has 80% committed. Lightspeed manages over $65B globally and has backed Anthropic, xAI, Databricks, and India's Sarvam AI.

**Google工程师因AI加速风险辞职，称进展"太快"**。Robert O'Callahan在Google从事芯片设计工具开发，他认为这些工具将加速AI芯片设计，使AI更便宜、更普及。他在辞职信中表示AI存在性风险应被认真对待，称"当前变革速度远超人类理解和适应的能力"，未来工作将"明确地亲人类"（unambiguously pro-human）。

**Google engineer resigns over AI acceleration concerns, says progress is "far too rapid"**。Robert O'Callahan worked on chip design tools at Google, which he says will accelerate AI chip development, making AI cheaper and more pervasive. In his resignation letter, he said existential risks deserve serious consideration, that "the current rate of change is far too high," and that his future work will be "unambiguously pro-human."

**DeepMind发布AlphaGenome Atlas：覆盖人类基因组90亿个单字母变异的预测图谱**。该数据集达1 petabyte，是AlphaFold Database的30多倍，并提供AVI score帮助研究人员快速排序变异影响。合作者已用其发现与 epileptic encephalopathy 相关的DNM1基因变异，以及22%更多非编码区遗传关联。

**DeepMind releases AlphaGenome Atlas: a predictive map of all 9 billion single-letter DNA variants**。The 1-petabyte dataset is over 30 times larger than the AlphaFold Database and includes an AVI score for rapid variant ranking. Collaborators used it to identify a DNM1 variant linked to epileptic encephalopathy and uncovered 22% more non-coding genetic associations.
