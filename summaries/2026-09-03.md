**1. Uber 在伦敦推出英国首批机器人出租车**

Uber has beaten Waymo to launch the UK's first commercial robotaxi service in London. The initial fleet consists of 15 Ford Mustang EVs with safety drivers, using Wayve's AI driving system that analyzes real-time data rather than following pre-programmed routes. Our journalist's test ride was mostly smooth, though the safety driver did intervene once when a van door swung open. Fully driverless service likely won't arrive until 2027 due to regulatory caution.

**2. Google 发布 Gemini 3.8 Flash 与网络安全专用模型 3.8 Flash Cyber**
Google 在六周内第三次发布 Flash 系列模型。3.8 Flash 以更低成本在 DeepSWE 编程基准上超越多数大型前沿模型；3.8 Flash Cyber 则专攻漏洞发现与自动修复，在 CyberGym 基准上得分86.2%，并能以低于前沿模型2.3~5.2倍的成本发现真实漏洞。Google 已用它保护自身代码——Cloud Vulnerability Research 团队在不到2小时内发现了一个通常需要数月才能找到的关键基础性漏洞。Cyber 版目前仅通过 Fairwind Program 向可信防御者开放。

Google has launched Gemini 3.8 Flash and a specialized cybersecurity variant, 3.8 Flash Cyber — the third Flash release in six weeks. The standard model outperforms larger frontier models on the DeepSWE coding benchmark at a fraction of the cost, while the Cyber version achieves 86.2% on CyberGym and discovers real-world vulnerabilities at 2.3–5.2x lower cost than leading frontier models. Google's Cloud Vulnerability Research found a critical foundational vulnerability in under 2 hours that would typically take months. The Cyber model is initially restricted to trusted defenders via the Fairwind Program.

**4. 全球近半数农民每年遭遇农药中毒**
一项发表在《Frontiers in Public Health》的研究发现，全球每年约有4.02亿至4.33亿农民和农场工人遭受意外急性农药中毒——占全球9.34亿农业人口的约46%。每年约11,000人因此死亡，其中近60%发生在印度。重度中毒率最高的是西非布基纳法索（约84%）。2023年全球农药使用量达380万吨，比1990年翻了一番。

A study in Frontiers in Public Health found that approximately 402–433 million farmers and farm workers suffer unintentional acute pesticide poisoning every year — about 46% of the global agricultural workforce of 934 million. Around 11,000 people die annually, with India accounting for nearly 60% of fatalities. Burkina Faso has the highest poisoning rate at ~84%. Global pesticide use reached 3.8 million tonnes in 2023, double the 1990 figure.

### IEEE Spectrum

**职业专栏：不同形式的导师制**
**核心观点**：你不需要「正式的」导师——观察、提问和不耻「抄袭」就能从前辈身上学到很多东西。

Author Brian Jenney (founder of career platform Parsity) argues you don't need a formal mentor — observation, curiosity, and shameless copying work. Directly asking "will you be my mentor?" is awkward; instead, figure out what great engineers do differently and "steal whatever seems useful." Great artists steal; engineers should too.

**AI 效率可能让我们失去下一代专家**
**核心观点**：当 AI 承担了原本培养专业技能的入门工作时，如果不刻意设计「人工关卡」来保留关键人类技能，企业将牺牲下一代工程师的专业能力。
作者曾在1980年代末为战斗机数字引擎控制器做验证工作，此前还参与过美国核电站全数字控制系统设计。哈佛大学的论文(覆盖2.8亿家公司、6500万工人)发现，采用生成式 AI 后，初级就业在六个季度内下降约9%，而高级就业继续增长。斯坦福对 ADP 数据的分析指向同一方向。无论是 AI 吸收初级工作还是远程工作切断师徒关系，本质是同一个破碎机制：专业知识从高级传递到初级的「学徒通道」。法航447号班机是经典教训：自动驾驶侵蚀了飞行员的手动飞行技能，导致一个本可恢复的情况变成了灾难。作者的处方是「人工关卡」——在工作流程中刻意设计需要人接管控制权的节点,不是为了效率,而是为了「锻炼和保留否则会衰退的技能」。
引文原文："You cannot become a senior engineer without first being a junior one. Expertise is not downloaded."

The author, who designed controls for a nuclear plant and verified fighter jet engine software, argues AI automation threatens the apprenticeship pipeline that builds expertise. Harvard research shows junior employment fell ~9% after genAI adoption while senior employment grew. His prescription: deliberately design "manual gates" in AI-augmented workflows — points where humans take control specifically to exercise skills that would otherwise decay, just as aviation mandates hand-flying practice. "You cannot become a senior engineer without first being a junior one. Expertise is not downloaded."
[原文链接](https://spectrum.ieee.org/ai-engineer-skills)
