**1. 美国将网络直播行刑队枪决 / US to livestream firing squad execution**

美国国防部长 Pete Hegseth 宣布，2009年胡德堡基地枪击案凶手、前陆军少校 Nidal Hasan 的死刑将以行刑队枪决方式执行，并将在网上直播。处决定于12月3日在得州胡德堡进行，这将是美国近一个世纪以来首次公开处决，也是二战后首次军事枪决。但法律专家指出，Hasan 的上诉程序尚未结束，处决很可能被联邦法院暂缓。Meta、TikTok 等主要平台均禁止直播处决内容，直播将在哪个平台进行仍是未知数。

US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth announced that the execution of Nidal Hasan, the former Army major convicted of killing 13 people at Fort Hood in 2009, will be carried out by firing squad and livestreamed. Scheduled for December 3 at Fort Hood, Texas, it would be the first public execution in the US in nearly a century and the first military firing squad execution since WWII. However, legal experts note Hasan's appeals process is not over and a federal court stay is likely. Major platforms including Meta and TikTok prohibit livestreaming executions, leaving the hosting platform uncertain.
