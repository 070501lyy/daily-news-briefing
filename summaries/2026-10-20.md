**为 AI agents 优化互联网可能让其他人更难受 / Optimising the web for AI agents may hurt everyone else**

**核心论点**：个人 AI agents 构成了人类与 bot 之外的第三类网络访问者，互联网可能被重塑为服务 agents 而非人类。

- OpenAI 的 Dots 与 Meta 的 Muse 让 agents 大规模上线，Muse 首月下载 500 万次，agentic 网络流量 8 月至 9 月增长两倍。
- 许多网站已封禁 agents，Amazon 封禁 Muse 并与 Perplexity 打官司，称 agents 威胁其广告业务；Shopify 则反其道为 agents 开设专属结账通道。
- Wharton 的 Ethan Mollick 预测，一旦 agents 被广泛用于退款和砍价，客服系统将被淹没至真人无法使用。
- 可能的后果包括：面向人类的界面日益劣化、频繁的人机验证（甚至虹膜扫描）、因人而异的动态定价、无法分辨推荐是否受广告影响。

**Core argument**: personal AI agents are a third category of web visitor, and the internet may be redesigned for them at humans' expense. Apps like OpenAI's Dots and Meta's Muse have pushed agents to scale, with Muse downloaded 5 million times in its first month and agentic traffic tripling from August to September. Many sites block agents; Amazon is litigating against Perplexity's while Shopify gives agents their own checkout lanes. Wharton's Ethan Mollick predicts agents will one day overwhelm customer service to the point real people can't use it. Possible downsides include degraded human interfaces, constant human verification, per-agent pricing and opaque recommendations. Whether that happens depends on rules still being written, such as whether businesses may block agents or stop them negotiating.

- 2025 年 Green Power Denmark 在部分运往逆变器的电路板上发现隐蔽通信通道；研究者还发现其父母太阳能板固件更新凭证公开暴露。
- Wood Mackenzie 估计欧盟到 2030 年需替换 4 至 5 GW 计划中的中国设备采购，约占同期计划采购的 14% 至 18%，可行但缓慢且昂贵。

**Core argument**: fears of grid backdoors are pushing the EU and US to squeeze Chinese components out of power equipment, both in new purchases and installed gear. From November 1, EU-funded grid projects will not buy inverters from "high-risk" suppliers. The US added inverters to FCC certification lists in July and issued an August executive order covering inverters, transformers, storage, generators and firmware, with DOE rule details due late December. Green Power Denmark found hidden communication channels on some circuit boards in 2025. Wood Mackenzie estimates the EU must replace 4-5 GW of planned Chinese purchases through 2030, about 14-18% of planned buying, which is possible but slow and costly. Fronius says switching could take a year and wants an outright ban; SMA Solar expects to gain about 10% share given its 2-4% China dependence.
