泄露的 Rosstat 数据显示，自 2022 年入侵乌克兰以来，至少 490,334 名俄罗斯士兵死亡；BBC Russian 比对 1100 万份死亡记录，发现 2025 年俄罗斯 18-24 岁男性死亡者中 65% 死于战争。数据还显示，西伯利亚贫困地区男性的战死率是莫斯科、圣彼得堡居民的 20-25 倍，另有至少 5,017 名外国人为俄作战身亡。在两座人口仅 74 人的小村庄争夺战中，俄军损失 1,122 人。

**AI data centers are turning to self-built gas plants**: as of October 2026, developers had announced more than 100 behind-the-meter gas projects in the US totaling over 115 GW, more than double California's peak demand. BloombergNEF estimates that if all are built, they would emit an extra 152 million metric tons of CO2e annually by 2035—more than the Netherlands' total emissions. Separately, NERC adopted its first reliability standards for computational loads, requiring data centers to disclose their power behavior.
