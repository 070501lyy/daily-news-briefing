**黄仁勋婚外情风波升级为美国国安事件。** 特朗普证实 FBI 正在调查英伟达 CEO 黄仁勋与疑似中国间谍黄乐（Alice Huang）之间的关系，并称黄仁勋对此“毫不知情”，他本人相信黄仁勋是“正直的人”。英伟达已启动独立调查，股价单日下跌逾 4%，市值蒸发约 2500 亿美元。加州还出现股东集体诉讼，指控黄仁勋与公司隐瞒相关关系。中国外交部称“中国间谍”说法“纯属捕风捉影，污蔑抹黑”。

US President Trump confirmed the FBI is investigating Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's affair with alleged Chinese spy Alice Huang. Trump said he asked Huang directly and believes he had "no idea she was a spy," calling him an "honourable guy." Nvidia has launched an independent investigation; shares fell over 4%, wiping roughly $250bn off its value. A shareholder class-action suit in California alleges Huang and the company concealed the relationship. China's foreign ministry called the spy claims "entirely groundless, speculative and intended to smear and malign China."

**中国前 10 个月贸易顺差首次突破 1 万亿美元。** 海关总署数据显示，10 月出口同比激增 35.2%，进口增长 41.6%，累计顺差达 1.04 万亿美元。数据中心设备与绿色能源产品是主要拉动项。但顺差扩大也折射内需疲软：房价五年下跌约 40%，家庭削减消费。对欧顺差前 10 个月达 3208 亿美元，加剧欧洲对产业失业的担忧。

China's trade surplus topped $1 trillion in the first ten months of the year, the first time it has surpassed that level. Exports jumped 35.2% year-on-year in October, driven by data-center gear and green-energy products. The surplus also reflects weak domestic demand: home prices have fallen about 40% over five years, prompting households to cut spending. The surplus with Europe reached $320.8bn over the period, heightening European fears of industrial job losses.

**加州“亿万富翁税”公投失败。** 选民否决了 Prop 40，即对亿万富翁大部分财富一次性征收 5% 的税，原本预计可为医保筹集 1000 亿美元。反对阵营（包括 Google 联合创始人 Sergey Brin）的竞选支出约为支持方的五到十倍。这场公投撕裂了加州民主党：州党部与 Bernie Sanders 支持，州长 Gavin Newsom 与候任州长候选人 Xavier Becerra 反对。

California voters rejected Proposition 40, a one-time 5% tax on billionaires' wealth that proponents said could raise $100bn for healthcare. Opponents, led by Google co-founder Sergey Brin, outspent supporters roughly 5–10 to 1. The measure split California Democrats: the state party and Bernie Sanders backed it, while Governor Gavin Newsom and gubernatorial candidate Xavier Becerra opposed it.

**英国政府网站曝出百余个严重安全漏洞。** 安全研究员 Chris Nesbitt-Smith 用消费级 Claude Opus 与 Sonnet 模型，在 33 个机构的 349 个公开代码库中约三分之二发现漏洞。最严重的是规划督察署（Planning Inspectorate）误将 Microsoft Entra ID 登录凭证提交至 GitHub，可获“神级”读写权限，涉及 HR 记录与法律保全证据。相关机构已修复。

Security researcher Chris Nesbitt-Smith used consumer-grade Claude Opus and Sonnet models to scan 349 public-sector codebases across 33 UK organizations, finding vulnerabilities in roughly two-thirds. The most serious: the Planning Inspectorate accidentally committed Microsoft Entra ID credentials to GitHub, granting "god-tier" read/write access to SharePoint sites, private OneDrive accounts, and legally held evidence. The agency says it detected the activity and fixed the issue within 20 minutes, with no data breach.

**乌克兰和平谈判在阿布扎比重启，首次三方会谈。** 美方官员称俄乌在顿涅茨克控制权问题上“私下展现了一点灵活性”，但双方公开立场仍相距甚远。谈判聚焦“战后安全架构”，可能包括安全磋商、危机热线与恢复北约-俄罗斯理事会。欧洲领导人要求加入谈判，法国外长强调英法德“一定会坐在谈判桌旁”。

Trilateral Ukraine peace talks resumed in Abu Dhabi, the first since February. US officials said Moscow and Kyiv have shown "a little bit of flexibility, privately" on who controls Ukraine's eastern regions, though public positions remain far apart. Talks center on a "postwar security architecture" that could include consultations, crisis hotlines, and reviving the NATO-Russia Council. European leaders demanded inclusion; France's foreign minister insisted the UK, France, and Germany "will indeed be in the meeting room."
