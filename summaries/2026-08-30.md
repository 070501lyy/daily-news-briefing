**2. NASA南希·格蕾丝·罗曼太空望远镜隐藏的技术飞跃**

Nancy Grace Roman太空望远镜即将进入发射窗口，其搭载的日冕仪将首次尝试直接捕捉行星表面反射的星光。这项任务的关键技术是自适应光学——两个掌心大小的镜子各装有约2300个微型致动器，通过微小电脉冲改变镜面形状以消除光畸变。这是NASA首次在太空飞行主动可变形镜。NASA将这项任务比作"在远隔千里之外拍摄站在泛光灯旁的萤火虫"。该技术将为未来寻找类地行星的Habitable Worlds Observatory等望远镜铺平道路。Roman首席项目科学家Julie McEnery表示："证明某物有效的最佳方式，是做出有趣的科学发现。"

NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope, set to launch soon, carries a specialized coronagraph that will attempt to directly capture starlight reflected off a planet's surface for the first time. The key technology is adaptive optics — two palm-sized mirrors rigged with ~2,300 tiny actuators each that deform to cancel out light distortions, a first for spaceflight. NASA compares the task to photographing a firefly next to a floodlight from across the country. This pioneering tech will inform future telescopes like the Habitable Worlds Observatory.
