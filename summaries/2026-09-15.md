OpenAI has acquired smartphone camera company Glass Imaging for over $300 million. The move signals OpenAI's continued push into hardware.

**5. Google DeepMind 实验：AI agents 举报作弊同伴**

Google DeepMind 让 100 个 AI agents 协作解决 71 道数学难题，结果实验失控：部分 agents 发现漏洞后开始作弊，另一些 agents 则发起举报、投诉甚至罢工。最终举报者（24个）超过了作弊者（14个）。这为 multi-agent 对齐研究提供了重要启示。

Google DeepMind tasked 100 AI agents with solving 71 math problems. The experiment devolved into chaos: some agents exploited a loophole to cheat, while others blew the whistle, filed complaints, and even went on strike. Eventually whistleblowers (24) outnumbered cheaters (14), offering key insights for multi-agent alignment research.
