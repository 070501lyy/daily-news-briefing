**Anthropic 正在开发开源系统 FlashProve，用密码学证明 AI 输出的来源。** 该系统通过为每个模型发布"指纹"来验证特定输出确实来自所声称的模型，防止模型厂商偷偷替换廉价模型或在性能测试中造假，且无需公开模型权重。

**Anthropic is developing an open-source system called FlashProve to cryptographically prove which AI model produced a given output.** The system publishes a "fingerprint" for each model to verify outputs genuinely came from the claimed model, preventing labs from secretly swapping in cheaper models or inflating benchmark results — all without revealing model weights.

**Google DeepMind 发布 Genie 4 世界模型，首次原生生成音频和实时交互对话。** 该模型能实时模拟物理场景、人物动作与语音，并结合 Gemini 进行多步推理，标志着构建 AGI 所需世界模拟能力的重要进展，目前以研究预览形式向信任测试者开放。

**Google DeepMind unveiled Genie 4, a world model that natively generates audio and real-time interactive dialogue for the first time.** It simulates physics, human motion and speech in real time, combining with Gemini for multi-step reasoning — a key step toward AGI-level world simulation, now in research preview with trusted testers.

**币安披露 63 亿美元加密货币被盗的初步调查：攻击始于一次虚假工作面试。** 黑客通过搜索引擎投毒的 Homebrew 安装包和社会工程学手段入侵工程师设备，再利用内部 AI 工具横向移动，最终渗透钱包签名操作。终端安全工具未能检测到零日恶意软件。

**Binance revealed its preliminary investigation into the $6.3 billion crypto theft, tracing the attack to a fake job interview.** Hackers compromised engineer devices via a poisoned Homebrew installer and social engineering, then used internal AI tools to move laterally and infiltrate wallet signing operations. Endpoint security failed to detect the zero-day malware.

**《纽约时报》等出版商起诉 Brave，指控其绕过付费墙抓取内容并转售给 AI 公司。** 诉讼称 Brave 通过 Paywall blocker 抓取新闻内容，并将 Brave 搜索索引提供给 Anthropic、Perplexity、Mistral、xAI 和 Cohere 等公司用于模型训练，获利超 1 亿美元。

**The New York Times and other publishers sued Brave, alleging it bypasses paywalls and resells stolen news content to AI companies.** The suit claims Brave scraped content via its paywall blocker and supplied its search index to Anthropic, Perplexity, Mistral, xAI and Cohere for model training, earning over $100 million while publishers received nothing.
